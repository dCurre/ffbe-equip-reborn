## Features (after the rework)
- **Copies by rarity (My Units cards):** each card shows a counter for every rarity the unit can have (`copiesByRarity`: 5★ ×1, 6★ ×0, 7★ ×1, NV EX0 ×1, NV EX1 ×2 ..., NV+ ×1) and the edit dialog has a field for each. `OwnedUnit.base` keeps the base copies by exact rarity (the import fills it from the last digit of the game id), `exCopies` the NV copies at EX0 to EX3 (from `exRank` of each exported copy; `exRank` is the highest) and `nvPlus` how many NV copies are NV+ (a subset of `nv`, the NV counters by EX add up to all of them). `base` and `exCopies` are ignored while they do not add up to `number` / `nv` (a fusion or STMR use leaves `base` out of date: then all the base copies count on the lowest rarity; without `exCopies` one NV copy is at the highest EX level and the others at EX0). The EX raise button takes the NV copy with the lowest EX level; removing a copy takes the lowest one too. Open: how an NV+ copy is marked in the game export is not known, so `nvPlus` is set by hand and an import replaces it (the whole collection is replaced); `useStmrSource` does not update `base`.
- **Unit card pills (My Units):** the TMR / STMR pills are buttons (a click marks one as farmed, the item line shows on hover; on a touch screen a long press shows it); the NV pills of each EX level add a copy at that level on a tap and remove one on a long press or right click.
