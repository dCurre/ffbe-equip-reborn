Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

FFBE Equip Reborn (https://dcurre.github.io/ffbe-equip-reborn/) is a free, unofficial fan tool for Final Fantasy Brave Exvius, maintained by dCurre. It is a static website: it has no server and no database of its own. This page explains what happens to your data when you use it.

Using the site without logging in

You can use the builder, the unit search, the encyclopedia and the other tools without logging in. The site does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

Your browser stores two small values locally (in localStorage): the theme you chose (light or dark) and, only if you log in, the Google access token and the session identifier described below. They never leave your device except as explained in this page.

Logging in with Google

Logging in is optional. It lets you save your units, inventory, espers, consumables and builder teams. Login uses Google Identity Services. The site asks Google for a single permission (drive.appdata): access to a hidden folder in your own Google Drive that only this application can use. The site does not request your email address, your name or access to any other file in your Google Drive or Google account.

Your data is stored as JSON files in that hidden folder, in your Google Drive, and is read and written directly by your browser. The maintainer of FFBE Equip Reborn cannot see it: there is no copy on any server of ours.

To keep you logged in until you log out, a small service of this site (a Cloudflare Worker) keeps the Google renewal token that Google gives at login. Your browser only holds a random session identifier and the access token (valid about one hour), which the service renews for you. The service never reads your Drive files. Logging out deletes the session, removes both values from your browser and revokes the renewal token at Google.

Deleting your data and removing access

  • To remove the site's access to your Google account, go to your Google Account → Security → third-party apps with account access (myaccount.google.com/permissions) and remove FFBE Equip Reborn.
  • To delete the data saved by the site, open Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps, find FFBE Equip Reborn and choose "Delete hidden app data".
  • To clear the values stored in your browser, clear the site data from your browser settings.

Third-party services

The site relies on these services, which receive the usual technical data of any web request (such as your IP address and browser type):

  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the site (GitHub Privacy Statement).
  • Google, only if you log in: Google Identity Services and the Google Drive API (Google Privacy Policy).
  • Public content delivery networks that serve the site's libraries, fonts and icons: cdnjs (Cloudflare), jsDelivr, code.jquery.com and Font Awesome.

Bug reports and suggestions sent from the Contribute page are created by you on GitHub, with your GitHub account, and are public.
